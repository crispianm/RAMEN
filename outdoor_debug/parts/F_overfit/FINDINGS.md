## Answers
1. One clip, distant geometry and sky: both are fit. On TA the released head starts out of domain: far pred:gt 0.145 -> 1.005, AbsRel 0.60 -> 0.020 in 300 steps. On VKITTI the head already has far pred:gt 0.97; AbsRel 0.062 -> 0.024. The loss does not reach ~0 (0.12 VKITTI, 0.07 TA, still falling); SiLog (0.043-0.048) decays slowly; the VDA part has a floor from the zero-disparity sky target (C1-04; measured VKITTI 0.075 is near that floor; C1's 0.066 figure is from a synthetic scene).
2. Baseline sky (hinge 0) is not pinned at a metric value. One clip: sky pulled monotonically farther, never saturates (VKITTI 652 -> 1335 m, TA 81 -> 1846 m, still rising at step 300): the zero-disparity target rewards pushing sky toward infinity, not holding it at hundreds of metres. Ten clips: baseline sky drifts DOWN while valid metrics improve (VKITTI 647 -> 556 m, TA 270 -> 174 m; share >= 400 m falls to 0.00 on TA). This reproduces, on a tiny controlled set with the identical recipe, the sky drift described in the SKY FIX comment in train.py.
3. The existing opt-in hinge fits sky under metric supervision: TA 10 clips sky 174 -> 505 m, share >= 400 m 0 -> 0.90, near fit unchanged (near-bin AbsRel 0.18 base vs 0.20 hinge, within noise; total loss 1.20 vs 1.16); VKITTI 10 clips sky 556 -> 758 m (0.81 -> 0.91). On 1-clip runs the hinge does little (sky already >400 m; hinge value 3.5e-3, 1e-4). The weighted hinge is only 0.005-0.007 at the end, 1-2 orders below the VDA loss, yet holds the sky. Neither clearly helps nor hurts far-valid geometry (TA far pred:gt 0.77 vs 0.63, oscillating 0.6-1.2 either way).
4. Far valid geometry on 10 clips is only partly fit in 600 steps: TA far pred:gt 0.63-0.77, far-bin AbsRel 0.28-0.41; VKITTI far-bin AbsRel 0.17 -> 0.14 (hinge 0.11), pred:gt 1.06-1.10. Curves noisy, still moving: "cannot" vs "slow" not separated.
5. Blocking term: head capacity is not it (one clip fits to AbsRel 0.02). Gradient clipping is unlikely (always active, head norm 3-6 vs 1.0, but AdamW largely cancels a uniform rescale; a clip-off run would be the single-factor test: NOT run). CONFIRMED: the missing absolute sky anchor: sky is outside SiLog, so only the VDA terms act on it at target disparity 0; baseline sky is unconstrained (runs away on 1 clip, drifts down on 10); a one-sided hinge fixes it at no measurable near-field cost. UNRESOLVED: whether the 1/d^2 gradient decay or the per-frame scale-and-shift freedom of the SSI term is responsible.
## Caveats: one seed per run; 10-clip curves noisy and 600 steps short (final fit not shown); not the full recipe (clean input, accum 1, no lr decay, no EMA): the claim is "can fit", not "will in the 12k-step run"; TA step 0 is out of domain for the released head (sky 81-270 m), so only VKITTI is an outdoor reference at step 0.
