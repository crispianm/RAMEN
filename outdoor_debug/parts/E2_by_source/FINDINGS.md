Scripts: grad_by_source.py (run), summarize.py, class_shares.py. Data: grad_by_source_rows.csv (10,628 rows: ckpt, batch, clip, source, group, comp, metric, value), batch_meta.json, summary_by_source.csv (pooled/per-batch/energy/projection shares, bootstrap CI over batches, per-clip norms), summary_massweighted.csv + summary_massweighted_grouped.csv (PRIMARY: mass-reweighted shares with clip-bootstrap CI), summary_by_source_class.csv (source x pixel class x loss), class_shares_by_mix.csv (pixel-class shares: true mix vs old local-6 mix), summary_grouped.csv. Scratch + run.log: /mnt/DATA/DegradedDepth/claude_outputs/scratch/outdoor_debug_20261016/E2_by_source/ (batches_seed45.pt, 20 batches, kept). Rerun from repo root: `PYTORCH_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True ~/.venvs/ramen/bin/python -I outdoor_debug/parts/E2_by_source/grad_by_source.py --out <dir>` (~35 min GPU for 3 models x 20 batches; <20 GB). GPU_LOCK taken/released by the script (left empty).
## Method (same as E_gradients, nothing re-implemented)
train.load_config of dn01_c3rep_20261009_ft_n46.yaml (FT/C3 loss blocks asserted equal), train._build_multisource with the real 9 sources and their sampling masses (tartanair 310000, vkitti 40000, PO train 87206, PO val 12794, irs 100000, spring 4843, kitti_raw 299397, nyu 170297, scannetpp 85149; WeightedRandomSampler seed 45), 20 distinct batches of 4 clips x 8 frames, identical across models, bf16 autocast forward in model.train(), activation-checkpointing monkeypatch, train._compute_loss_vda re-called per term (VDA = silog weight 0; SiLog = full - VDA; sky term = VDA(sky on) - VDA(sky off)). Gradients are vjps of the pred-map gradient (loss depends on params only through pred); per-clip = vjp of the single clip's pred-gradient (exact separation); per-class = same with the class mask. Models: released Metric-VDA-S (FT architecture), FT n46 step12000, RAMEN n46 step12000 (trainable_ema). Seed-45 realised draw is NOT the mix (80 clips): n_clips tartanair 26, vkitti 5, PO-train 3, PO-val 1, irs 12, spring 0, kitti 17, nyu 13, scannetpp 3 (spring 0.4% mass has no clip; PO/ScanNet++ under-drawn, IRS over-drawn). Therefore the PRIMARY numbers are MASS-REWEIGHTED: share_s = mass_s * mean_clip_value_s / sum_s' mass_s' * mean_clip_value_s' (CI: bootstrap of clips within source, 500 draws). Raw pooled shares (what E reported) are also in the CSVs. "Share" = share of the sum of per-clip parameter-gradient L2 norms (output_conv = the head's final conv group, "all" = all trainable params); projection share = <clip grad, batch total>/|total|^2, mean over batches.
## Data coverage (what is local; the NYU and ScanNet++ shares are therefore approximate)
- NYU: 3 of 12 train shards (train-000000..2, never val-*) = 101 of 291 scene dirs (35%), 417 of 1307 clips (32%). The other 9 shards are not on this host. Each local clip carries its true mass weight (mass is per source, not per clip), so the NYU SOURCE share is exact in mass terms; only the scene diversity of its mean clip norm is estimated from 35% of the scenes.
- ScanNet++: config = splits [sem_test] under scannetpp_train + extra subset140 under scannetpp_train_full. Local: the 140 subset140 scenes from scannetpp_train_full (all present, extra_scene_list sha pinned, eval-disjointness asserts passed); the 50 sem_test scenes live only under data/TEST/ScanNet++ and were NOT opened (held-out TEST data rule): 140 of 190 scenes (74%), 42,093 of 54,585 clips (77%). Same remark as NYU.
- All other 7 sources: complete local roots (PO keep lists filtered to local dirs as in E).

## Direct answers
(a) KITTI. NO: with the indoor sources in the mix KITTI still carries only 6.7% [4.7-8.8] of the head gradient norm (FT; RAMEN 7.1%, released 8.3%) and 10.3% of the all-params norm, against 27.0% of the sampling mass (ratio 0.25 head, 0.38 all-params). The earlier local-source result (7-8% of head norm vs 27% mass) is CONFIRMED on the true mix, not an artefact of the missing indoor data (re-reweighting the same run to the old local-6 mix gives KITTI 8.9%, so the indoor sources dilute it only slightly further). Mechanism, unchanged: a KITTI clip is 19% valid pixels (sparse LiDAR; 5.3% of valid pixels in the sampled batches, 7.3% mass-reweighted) and its mean head norm is 0.35 vs 1.6 for TartanAir (0.22x); gradient share / valid-pixel share = 0.92, i.e. per valid pixel KITTI counts about as much as TartanAir, it just has few valid pixels per clip. Its projection onto the batch total is negative (-3% FT, -2.6% RAMEN, -2.1% released; SiLog part -4.9% FT): in a batch with KITTI, its head gradient points slightly against the sum of the others. VDA alone gives KITTI 9.4% (FT; 15.6% released), SiLog alone 6.4%. KITTI near-class (<10 m) VDA gradient is 36% of that class's norm, but its SiLog share of near pixels only 2.6%.
(b) Indoor sources (IRS, NYU, ScanNet++). Mass-reweighted share of the head gradient norm, FT / RAMEN / released: SiLog 43.8% / 42.1% / 37.2%, full loss 43.0% / 41.4% / 37.2%, VDA 38.9% / 38.5% / 37.6%. All-params: SiLog 38.4 / 36.2 / 33.1%, full 36.4 / 35.3 / 31.8%, VDA 34.0 / 35.3 / 31.6%. Reference: indoor mass 32.0%, valid pixels 44.0%. So indoor takes 1.3x its mass and ~its pixel share; ranking IRS (18.0% head, 2.0x its mass) > NYU (14.6%, ~mass) > ScanNet++ (10.4%, 1.36x). Raw (unreweighted) pooled: 47.4% FT (IRS over-drawn in this seed). All three are metric (SiLog) sources with 10 m caps. Per-source indoor SiLog (head, FT): IRS 18.7%, NYU 15.0%, ScanNet++ 10.2%; NYU and ScanNet++ SiLog projections onto the batch total are NEGATIVE or ~0 (NYU -18% FT, ScanNet++ -12% FT: batches with them point partly against the TartanAir/IRS sum), IRS +37%, TA +97% (SiLog, FT).
(c) Far-valid and sky: yes, they shrink further, modestly. Head-norm share of the summed class norms (mass-reweighted, FT; old local-6 mix -> true mix): SiLog far 80-655 m 6.1% -> 4.7%, mid 10-80 m 19.3% -> 14.9%, near 74.6% -> 80.4%; VDA far 80-655 2.0% -> 1.8%, valid >=655 m 0.12% -> 0.11%, sky 4.8% -> 4.3%, near 54.9% -> 58.9%. All-params: SiLog far 8.1% -> 6.6%, VDA far 2.5% -> 2.2%, VDA sky 5.8% -> 5.1%. RAMEN the same within ~0.3 points. Pred-map L1 share of the clip total (FT, true mix): SiLog far 0.12%, VDA far <0.005%, VDA sky 0.04% (old mix 0.17%, <0.01%, 0.07%): the far/sky pixels carry almost no first-order pred-map gradient and less with indoor present. Sky-term (zero-disparity sky) head norm / full-loss head norm: 4.7% FT, 3.6% RAMEN, 6.2% released (old local-6 mix 5.3%, 4.1%, 7.4%); / SiLog norm 5.2% (5.9% old). Note E reported 14-17% for the sky term vs SiLog on output_conv; that was a batch-level ratio on the 9 sky-containing batches only, whereas this is a mass-weighted mean over all clips (most clips have no sky): do not compare the two directly. Far (80-655 m) gradient comes only from TartanAir (82% of the VDA far-class norm) and VKITTI (18%); sky only from TartanAir (94%) and VKITTI (6%): real outdoor sources contribute no far or sky supervision at all (KITTI max_depth 80, no sky label; PO no sky label). Indoor sources add nothing there by construction, they only dilute the far/sky fraction of the sampled mass (far 80-655 m px = 1.5% of all pixels in the true mix vs 1.9% local-6).
(d) TartanAir dominance: NOT robust as stated. On the true mix TartanAir carries 31.9% [23-41] of the head norm (FT; 31.3% RAMEN, 34.0% released) and 35.8% of the all-params norm, i.e. 1.14x its 27.9% mass: still the single largest source and the only one that is also the main far/sky provider, but not the 55% majority. The 55% of E was inflated by the missing indoor data (removing NYU/ScanNet++ gives 42.5% on the same batches mass-reweighted, 34.8% raw pooled here) and by that seed's draw. Corrected picture: TA ~32% head (~36% all), IRS ~18% (2x its mass), NYU ~15%, PO ~15% (CI 3-28, 4 clips only), ScanNet++ ~10%, KITTI ~7%, VKITTI ~3.5%. The qualitative conclusions that survive: (i) KITTI is strongly under-represented per unit mass (0.25x); (ii) IRS is over-represented (2x); (iii) outdoor far/sky gradients are carried by TartanAir/VKITTI alone. TartanAir's CI overlaps IRS/PO so "TA dominates" is a statement about the largest single source, not about a majority.
## Caveats
80 clips per model (identical across the three models); NYU 35% / ScanNet++ 74% of scenes (sem_test not opened); PO-train 3 clips, PO-val 1 clip, spring 0 clips (CIs for them are not informative); 8-frame clips; first-order gradients at fixed trained weights, not training dynamics; share-of-summed-clip-norms is not the norm of the summed gradient (the projection column gives the latter); SiLog pixel count equals valid pixel count in the sampled data here (the 655 m ceiling removes only <1% of TartanAir valid pixels).
