## Direct answers to the PLAN checkboxes
- Loss space: disparity. Metric depth `pred` -> `1/clamp(pred,0.1,1e5)` (metric_depth_clamp) once; GT -> `1/depth` on mask only, 0 elsewhere. No reciprocal of zero (mask*(depth>0), clamp_min 1e-3, finite assert). Sky: target 0 only for `sky>0.5 & ~mask` and only when `sky_vda_zero_disparity` (true in C3/FT).
- Sky in normalisation: yes when the zero-disparity switch is on (it is in the mask), it changes the mean-abs-dev normaliser (C1-14) and the loss floor (perfect predictions 0.0656, C1-04).
- Normalisation: SSI/gradient per frame, TGM per clip scale+shift; no per-batch. It removes metric scale entirely (C1-01); only SiLog pins it.
- Weighting: pixel-pooled over the whole batch in every term (not clip- or sample-weighted); the sparse/dense split is sample-share weighted (n_sp/n_all) between the two groups.
- SiLog: DA-V2 formula, weight 1.0, lambda 0.5, raw metres, `metric_sup` survives collate and restricts the term to supervised sources (Spring only is false), per-source 655 ceiling on TA/PO, no global ceiling; sky never in SiLog (outside `mask`); far geometry < 655 m included; 150-400 m not excluded.
- Sky gradient under the effective config: non-zero from the VDA loss only, correct direction, small and decaying with depth; zero from SiLog.
- Temporal: TGM masks require both frames valid (+ the 5% range filter); with sky in the mask the sky must be temporally stable at (scaled) disparity 0.
