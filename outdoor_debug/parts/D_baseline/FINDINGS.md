## Findings
1. Far/sky level is 2-4x nearer in all our models: a shift in LEVEL, not saturation. KITTI DEV, all frames with sky, clean: median sky depth release 479 m, FT n46 191 m, RAMEN s45 156 m, RAMEN n46 155 m. VKITTI (true sky label 655 m): release 576 m, ours 296-321 m. Every model puts 81-98% of sky pixels above 80 m; share of sky nearer than the scene's GT p95 is the same on KITTI (18%). RAMEN and FT behave alike => shared-recipe effect.
2. The user's KITTI drive_0023 frame 109 llb3 case reproduces (sky mask 13,938 px). median / p10 / p90 / >80 m / >200 m: Released 454 / 374 / 527 / 100% / 100%; C3 s45 144 / 94 / 174 / 100% / 0%; RAMEN n46 201 / 113 / 257 / 100% / 50%; FT n46 155 / 107 / 198 / 100% / 10%. (User's unaudited render: 442.6 vs 132.7 m: consistent.) Clean frame 109 medians 591 / 191 / 184 / 242 m. FT is not systematically nearer than RAMEN (farthest of ours on clean f109 and over all KITTI frames); within-ours variation (144-201 m) is as large as FT-vs-RAMEN; release-vs-ours is 2-4x. Per-frame sky medians over all KITTI DEV frames with sky: clean release 458 m vs ours 159-202 m; llb3 448 vs 144-168. ~24% of frames in every model have sky medians of 10-20 m: colour-rule false positives on bright smooth non-sky regions, the same frames for all models.
3. Horse real clip (51 frames, 518 short side, cubic; RAMEN n46 and FT n46 reproduce the dump to <=0.4 m): sky band (top 15%) median (p10-p90) / frame 25 / whole-frame max / tree-line band (rows 38-46%) / ground band (bottom 10%): Released 630 (616-637) / 631 / 679 / 226 / 6.8 m; C3 s45 220 (210-226) / 220 / 242 / 85 / 4.9; RAMEN n46 200 (192-205) / 201 / 228 / 82 / 3.7; FT n46 228 (220-236) / 230 / 249 / 85 / 4.0. Release ~3x farther in the sky and 2.7x at the tree line.
4. No saturation/hard ceiling in ours: predictions exactly 0 or <0.1 m are 0 for every model and dataset; share within 1% of the observed max <= 0.02%; horse sky flat across frames (per-frame medians 196-222 m, ~90-94% of frame max = the sky itself): a flat, softly bounded sky, not a clamp. On VKITTI ours keep rising with GT beyond 80 m (~200 m at GT 200, 350-400 at GT 650: mild under-estimate); the release tracks GT better (~480 m at GT 650). p99.9: ours 380-560 m, release ~667-672 m; the release behaves as if it has a ceiling near 650 m, ours max out at 417-617 m and vary by clip.
5. Scored-metric picture: KITTI raw pooled AbsRel release 0.130 vs ours 0.143-0.148 (release better at 0-30 m; ours better at 30-80 m: 0.176-0.188 vs 0.222; aligned-scale 0.083-0.085 ours vs 0.107 release). VKITTI >80 m raw: AbsRel ours 0.21-0.24 vs release 0.277; RMSE 49-53 m vs 70 m; delta1 0.51-0.56 vs 0.63 (release better on delta1). Horizon band: non-sky median over-estimated 15-20% by every model (KITTI pred/GT median 1.20 ours, 1.15 release): no clear model difference. Indoor raw AbsRel 0.112-0.116 ours vs 0.266 release.
## Caveats: the cause of the nearer sky/far field is not established here; no harm to a scored metric is shown (KITTI sky has no GT; on VKITTI >80 m ours are not worse than the release on AbsRel/RMSE); head saturation, near-zero predictions and a horizon-band difference are not supported.
