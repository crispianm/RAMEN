## Method
Real train.load_config (FT and C3 loss blocks asserted identical), train._build_multisource (real datasets, sampling: mass, per-source silog ceilings, sparse flags), default collate, build_model + each checkpoint's trainable_ema, bf16 autocast forward in model.train(). Every loss component is a re-call of the real train._compute_loss_vda with one switch changed (nothing re-implemented). VDA total = metric_silog_weight 0; SiLog = full - VDA (exact); sky term = VDA(sky_vda_zero_disparity on) - VDA(off) (includes the knock-on through the per-frame normalisers). Parameter gradients = vjps of the pred-map gradients (exact: the loss depends on params only through pred). Checkpoints: released Metric-VDA-S (FT architecture at vda_init released), FT n46, RAMEN n46, C3 s45 (step12000; no intermediate checkpoints locally). 20 distinct train batches of 4 clips x 8 frames (16 frames OOMs at 24 GB; RAMEN/C3 used activation checkpointing as a runtime monkeypatch, gradient differs ~2e-5 relative), identical across models, seed 45. Sources on disk: TartanAir, VKITTI, PointOdyssey-train, IRS, Spring, KITTI-raw; NYU/ScanNet++ NOT on this host (23% of true mass: local shares ~1.3x true; indoor-metric gradient not measured). Sky labels only exist for TartanAir/VKITTI: 11 of 20 batches have no sky pixel, sky statistics use the 9 that do. Pixel classes (all models): near <10 m 49.9%, mid 10-80 m 15.5%, far valid 80-655 m 2.7%, valid >=655 m 3.0%, sky 3.6%, invalid 25.2%.
## Direct answers
(a) Sky pixels get a finite, non-zero gradient that reaches the metric head, only where a sky label exists. Non-finite fraction 0 everywhere; SiLog's gradient on sky is exactly 0 (outside its mask); invalid pixels (25%: KITTI beyond-LiDAR and unlabelled sky) get exactly 0 from every term. Sky-pixel-only parameter-gradient L2 (4 models, 9 sky batches): 0.16-0.23 output_conv, 0.22-0.30 last_dpt, 0.37-1.03 all trainable.
(b) Per pixel (L1 per pixel per log-depth, FT n46, sky batches, full gradient): near 1.6e-6, mid 1.3e-6, far 80-655 m 5.8e-7, far >=655 m 1.1e-7, sky 1.5e-7 (released 3.0e-7): sky ~10x below near, 3.8x below far. Pred-map L1 share: sky 0.6%, far 1.4%, mid 18%, near 80%. Parameter norms FT n46 (all / output_conv): full 4.69 / 2.44; VDA 2.34 / 0.97; SiLog 3.81 / 2.27; VDA from sky pixels 0.43 / 0.17; VDA from near 3.24 / 2.04; VDA from far 80-655 0.55 / 0.30; sky term (with knock-on) 0.89 / 0.38. The sky term is 14-17% of SiLog's norm on output_conv (23-30% over all params). ~97% of the sky term's pred-map L1 mass sits on VALID pixels: zero-disparity sky acts mostly through the per-frame normaliser. SiLog on far geometry is large per pixel (4.1e-7 vs 1.2e-7 near); VDA on far is 6.7x lower per pixel than near; SiLog/VDA L1 on far-valid = 1.7-2.0x (FT/RAMEN/C3; 1.2x released), 0.08x near, 0.12x mid. Valid >=655 m pixels (TartanAir/PointOdyssey, 3.0%) get VDA only (SiLog 0); models predict ~140 m there, median pred/gt 0.03.
(c) Far-valid: VDA and SiLog agree in the head (cosine 0.62-0.89 output_conv, 0.58-0.77 all params, n=14). Near/mid weakly aligned (0.11-0.32); over all pixels ~orthogonal (output_conv -0.08..0.01): scale-invariant VDA and metric SiLog act on different directions. Pixel space: far-valid cosine 0.18-0.28 (62-66% sign agreement), no systematic conflict. Sky: no VDA/SiLog conflict possible (SiLog sky gradient is 0); the sky-pixel VDA gradient is aligned with far geometry (+0.46..+0.60 all params) and anti-aligned with near (-0.22..-0.29, noisy). One-step lookahead (one SGD step lr 1e-2 on a bit-exactly restored copy, 9 sky batches): sky-term step norm 0.004-0.015 (full gradient 0.05-0.09); SiLog change from the sky-term step FT +0.0033+-0.013, RAMEN +0.0078+-0.022, C3 +0.0121+-0.017, released +0.0215+-0.053 (within noise); median pred/gt of far-valid moves +0.039/+0.010/+0.032/+0.001 (toward GT; baseline 0.63/0.66/0.57/0.49); near ~-0.01; sky median depth +5/-0.6/+3/+29 m (baseline 183/180/149/315 m). So the sky term is a small push that does not damage valid depth beyond noise at first order (C1-04's compression prediction not visible; far-valid sign opposite). Matched-norm steps (norm0.3) overshoot for every direction: not interpretable, not used. Baseline context on train batches: median predicted depth on labelled sky 149-183 m trained (released 315 m); 32-41% of sky pixels predicted nearer than 80 m; far-valid (80-655 m) pred/gt 0.49 released vs 0.57-0.66 trained; near <10 m 1.28 released vs 1.07-1.09 trained.
(d) TartanAir dominates the head gradient. Share of output_conv gradient (FT / RAMEN): TartanAir 56% / 55%, PointOdyssey 20% / 20%, IRS 14% / 15%, KITTI 7.4% / 7.9%, Spring 1.3%, VKITTI 1.2% / 1.0%; all-params TartanAir 54-56%, KITTI 11%. TartanAir is also 54% of valid pixels and SiLog pixels, 41% of clips. By loss: SiLog TartanAir 57%, PO 21%, IRS 15%, KITTI 6%, VKITTI 1%; VDA TartanAir 47-49%, PO 16-18%, KITTI 16%, IRS 12-13%, Spring 5%.
(e) KITTI does NOT contribute what its sampling mass implies: 28.7% of sampled clips (27% true mass) but 8.2% of valid pixels (20% valid per clip vs 87-99% dense), 8.9% of SiLog pixels, 7-8% of head output_conv gradient norm (11% all-params); projection share onto the total head gradient ~0 or negative (-5% FT, -3% RAMEN; -6% for its SiLog part: in batches containing KITTI its SiLog head gradient points against the sum of the other sources'); per-clip SiLog L1 mass 0.0017-0.0021 vs 0.05-0.07 for TartanAir/PO (30-40x smaller). Under VDA KITTI is more present (16% output_conv, 19-23% all). Caveats: 14 of 20 batches contain KITTI; NYU/ScanNet++ absent.
