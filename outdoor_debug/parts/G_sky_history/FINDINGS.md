## 0. Headline (5 facts)
1. [DOC] The hinge was the USER's deliberate sky fix (K76, 2026-10-01 18:08 BST, V2 picked from a V1-V4 screen), added because training with the 80 m cap left the sky ~0 m-ish: pre-fix fv2_s KITTI sky_ge80 = 0.002 vs released 0.72 (DD/audit/SKY_screen_descriptive_column_20261001.md s.1). It restored sky to ~released level but cost ~+2.5% KITTI unaligned AbsRel. DD/audit/conflicts.md:533; DD/claude_outputs/reports/sky_pick_record_20261001.md.
2. [DOC] There is NO single "set hinge 0.1 -> 0" decision. 0.0 first appears as one of the arm keys of the 4090 SKYSCALE ladder (C1 hinge off; C2 = zero-disparity sky + hinge 0; C3 = C2 + cap lift + SiLog on TA/PO), pre-declared 2026-10-07 10:45Z after the user asked "didn't we add the hinge loss to FIX the sky problem? ... I think the 80m cap is still dodgy" (DD/audit/SKYSCALE_4090_screen_predeclared_20261007.md). Motivation: BUGHUNT R1 + METRICFIX A5 (hinge-off took option-B night T 2.66 -> 1.09, "sky NOT measured"). C3 = bfix arm `dn01_v3_bfix_C3_B` (commit c1abf81, 2026-10-08 14:30) was the "declared numerical pick" of the B-FIX read and was locked as the architecture (ARCH_LOCK_20261012.md:23). The hinge was replaced by sky at target disparity 0 (`sky_vda_zero_disparity: true`), NOT simply removed. Nobody ran hinge 0.1 vs 0.0 as a one-factor ablation on the final recipe.
3. [DOC] Closest 0.1-vs-0 evidence: (a) 4090 screen on r50 mvdas_ft, 1440 steps: C0 (hinge 0.1, cap 80) KITTI sky_med 88.7 m / sky_ge80 0.513 vs C1 (hinge 0, no sky term) 51 m / 0.137 vs C2 (zero-disp, hinge 0) 149.5 m / 0.657 vs C3 147.7 m / 0.695 vs released 344 m / 0.723; (b) 12k Isambard fixscreen: hinge 0.1 everywhere in R0 gives KITTI sky_med 94 m; R3 (hinge metric-only + zero-disp) 176 m; (c) B-FIX: Rx (hinge 0.1 metric-only + zero-disp + cap 655, NO SiLog on TA/PO) vs C3 (hinge 0, zero-disp, cap 655, SiLog on TA/PO): idx28 0.576086 vs 0.575809, outdoor-9 0.828337 vs 0.799010, night unaligned 0.356018 vs 0.266199 (+TAG). Rx vs C3 differs in TWO factors (hinge AND TA/PO SiLog coverage), so it cannot isolate the hinge.
4. [DOC] Mechanism seen for hinge-0.1 under cap 80: hinge min_depth (80 m default) == the 80 m training cap, so sky piles up at 81-95 m (released ~408 m) (BUGHUNT_20261007.md:7,Auditor correction). The hinge has no counter-force on non-metric clips (TartanAir/PO without SiLog): METRICFIX A5 found it "CAUSE-PARTIAL" (R 0.746) for the night scale inflation.
5. [DOC] The current C3 recipe's sky is still ~150-210 m median (KITTI), vs released ~345-408 m; FT n46/47/48 and C3rep n46/47/48 are equal on sky (sky_med 184-213; A_sky REPORT), tae_gtvalid unaffected by sky. C3's sky_ge80 on the 4090 ladder was only 0.695 (released 0.723): sky is NOT "near-constant far" for either FT or RAMEN. [INF] Hinge alone (min 80) cannot reproduce released's 400 m; a two-sided/higher floor (sky_hinge_min_depth ~200-300) was only tried once (S1, min 200, a1h family; see section 3).

## 4. Why 0.1 -> 0.0: what is documented
- No sentence "hinge dropped because X" exists for C3. Documented reasons: (i) hinge makes night scale drift farther (METRICFIX A5, BUGHUNT R1), (ii) hinge floor == cap (80) so sky piles at 81-95 m, (iii) the SKYSCALE ladder pre-declared C2/C3 as "sky at disparity 0, hinge 0" (June fix V1 as the replacement), (iv) B-FIX read picked C3 on night error (0.266 vs Rx 0.356) and the user approved the architecture (D1). [DOC]
- [INF] The hinge was dropped as part of a bundle, never ablated alone on the final recipe; the choice of C3 over Rx rests on the TA/PO SiLog supervision (which in C3 also adds a counter-force to any scale push).

- Arm H: c3rep seed-46 C3 recipe (`dn01_c3rep_20261009_c3_n46`, from scratch, 12k, same data stream) + `loss.sky_hinge_weight: 0.1`; everything else unchanged. Comparator: c3rep n46 (already scored). - Key interactions to handle (document in the config header, do not change them in the same run):
  1. `sky_hinge_min_depth` default 80 (train.py:1182); with 655 supervision caps the hinge floor is not at the cap; keep 80 for the one-factor test (equals the V2 setting), and run a follow-up with min 200/300 (S1 precedent) only after.
  2. `sky_hinge_metric_only` default false: in C3 all train sources are now metric-supervised (TA/PO added), so metric_only is equivalent to all-sky; the "no counter-force" concern from A5 no longer applies to TA/PO (SiLog 655 on them). [INF]
  3. `check_sky_loss` requires hinge weight > 0 for metric_only / max_depth (train.py:1484); `sky_hinge_max_depth` (two-sided band) unused: leave null.
  4. Hinge acts on the raw prediction; zero-disp acts on the SSI/grad/TGM mask. SiLog uses the ORIGINAL mask (train.py:1152). Keep sky_vda_zero_disparity true (otherwise it is a 2-factor change).
  5. L3 normaliser caveat on far-sky TA clips (ARCH_LOCK:125) applies equally to both arms (do not read L3-related rows).
  6. Hinge nonzero count on the 4090 smoke: 87 of 320 calls nonzero (max 2.95) at cap 80 and C0 recipe (tmp_skyscale/GATES.md); expect much sparser nonzero values with 655 supervision since C3 sky sits at ~148 m median; check hinge firing via `loss.log_terms: true` (sky_hinge_weighted) in a 20-step smoke before launching.
