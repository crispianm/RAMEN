## Question
Does correcting TartanAir sky labels (TS v2), and then adding a one-sided sky hinge, move far/sky depth toward GT when applied from the c3_n46 step-12000 checkpoint? Note from the trajectory: compression appears by step 6000 and plateaus, so this screen tests recovery from a settled model, not origin. A negative result at 2000 steps therefore does NOT rule out applying the fix from init.
Caveat on the target: VKITTI trains on all 5 scenes and the TartanAir envs are training envs, so the primary far/sky metrics ("training scenes") measure fit to the corrected labels, not generalisation. DEV has no held-out far/sky GT. KITTI DEV unaligned AbsRel is the real-scene guard; horse/KITTI sky medians are description only. ## TS v2 exact rule (updated 2026-10-17T17:35Z after Auditor O12; files in parts/TA_sky/, hashes in TA_sky_followup.SUMS; AWAITING Auditor re-check)
TS2 is ONE arm (all of the following are its sub-changes) versus the released loader (c1abf81, unchanged). No whole env is excluded.
1. Seg-sky replaces saturation as the sky definition (id-0 guard: id 0 is sky only where saturated; finite id 0 is uncertain).
2. Saturated non-sky pixels (inf, NaN, 65504, d >= 1e4) are UNCERTAIN (sky=0, mask=0), unless the env has `sat_nonsky_is_sky` (SoulCity).
3. The saturation test is inclusive at 1e4 (10000.0 is no longer dropped from both sets).
4. Far-dome guard (74-env class: 43 far, 14 near, 17 no seg-sky; median seg-sky depth >= 9000 m = far; gap clean, 7584 vs 11512 m): far-dome sky = seg_sky & (saturated | d >= 9000); finite seg-sky below 9000 m is uncertain.
5. Near-dome envs (seg-sky at any depth is sky) only if the visual gate `seg_sky_ok` is true; otherwise all seg-sky is uncertain. seg_sky_ok false (7): RetroOffice, AbandonedCable, CarWelding, Hospital, OldBrickHouseDay, CountryHouse, SoulCity. True: 14 others (e.g. Downtown, AmusementPark, Prison, ShoreCaves, GreatMarsh, Antiquity3D).
6. Unlabelled-env fallback (extension rule, O10a, applied uniformly to all 15): ON (8) ArchVizTinyHouseNight, FactoryWeather, SeasonalForestWinterNight, JapaneseAlley, OldTownFall, OldTownSummer, OldTownWinter, SeasideTown; uncertain (7) CoalMine, Ocean, OldBrickHouseNight, OldTownNight, Sewerage, Supermarket, HongKong. The strict-rule pass (3 ON) is kept in the exploration record. The 'interior' clause is removed.
7. Antiquity3D and MiddleEast saturated non-sky stays uncertain (0 informative frames); SoulCity and ArchVizTinyHouseNight saturated px kept as sky (open sky in all informative frames).
8. OldTownNight is a stated RESIDUAL (sky is a 100-230 m ramp, no clean plateau; released behaviour sky=0, mask=1).
Mask = isfinite & d>0 & d<max_depth & ~sky & ~uncertain.
