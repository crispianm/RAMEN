## TS v2 exact rule (updated 2026-10-17T17:12:02Z; Auditor O10 C1-C6; files in parts/TA_sky/; AWAITING Auditor re-check of this section)
TS2 is ONE arm with THREE sub-changes versus the released loader (c1abf81, unchanged):
1. Seg-sky at any depth (finite-dome fix): sky = seg class in the env's sky ids at any depth, so finite domes (e.g. Downtown 233-510 m, AmusementPark, Prison, ShoreCaves, Hospital, GreatMarsh; released rule catches ~0% there) become sky=1, mask=0.
2. Labelled envs, saturated and seg non-sky (incl. the 9.4-10 km edge of the ~11.6 km domes): UNCERTAIN (sky=0, mask=0) instead of valid.
3. Unlabelled envs (14 with no sky name, + ArchVizTinyHouseNight whose skysphere id is absent): per the frozen visual table unlabelled_decisions.csv (frame rule q=0.2/0.5/0.8 of the first trajectory; fallback_sat_is_sky=True only if the saturated px are open sky in ALL 3 frames; else uncertain). Decision ON: FactoryWeather, SeasonalForestWinterNight, ArchVizTinyHouseNight (interior view through windows; sky in all 3 frames; Auditor to confirm). OFF/uncertain: the other 12. This REMOVES the released zero-disparity sky supervision on saturated px in those 12 envs.
Rule: saturated = ~isfinite(d) | d >= 1e4; mask = isfinite & d>0 & d<max_depth & ~sky & ~uncertain. No whole env is excluded.
